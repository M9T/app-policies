Luxlow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 April 2026

Luxlow is a screen dimmer app developed by m9t. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Luxlow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no internet access and does not communicate with any external server.

Local Storage

The app stores your preferences (brightness level, color temperature, schedule times, presets) locally on your device using Android DataStore. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps — used solely to render the dimming overlay on your screen. No content of other apps is read or recorded.
  • Foreground service — keeps the overlay active while you use other apps. No background data processing occurs.
  • Receive boot completed — optionally restarts the overlay after device reboot, if enabled by the user.

Third Parties

Luxlow contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising frameworks, or crash reporting tools.

Children

The app does not collect any data from any users, including children.
