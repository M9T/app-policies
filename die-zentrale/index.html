Die Zentrale – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2026

Die Zentrale is a companion app for fans of Die drei Fragezeichen, developed by m9t. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Die Zentrale does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app has no internet access and does not communicate with any external server.

Local Storage

The app stores your episode history and listening insights locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with anyone.

Third Parties

Die Zentrale contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising frameworks, or crash reporting tools.

Children

The app does not collect any data from any users, including children.
