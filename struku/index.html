Privacy Policy

Streakly · Last updated: May 2026

Short version: Streakly does not collect any personal data, does not send data to external servers, and does not track you. All your data stays exclusively on your device.

1. Controller

This app is provided by an independent developer.
Contact: kontakt@streakly.app

2. What data is processed?

Streakly only processes data you enter yourself:

  • Names and settings of your habits
  • Date and time of completed habits
  • Preferred notification times (derived from your usage behavior)

This data is stored exclusively on your device in a local SQLite database.

3. Data sharing

Streakly does not transmit any data to external servers, third parties, analytics services, or any other recipients. The app does not communicate with the internet.

4. Notifications

Streakly can display local notifications on your device to remind you about your habits. These notifications:

  • are scheduled locally on your device (no external push service)
  • contain no personal data
  • can be disabled at any time in your device's system settings

5. System permissions

Streakly requires the following Android permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To display habit reminder notifications.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To reschedule notifications after a device reboot.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To trigger notifications at the precise scheduled time.

None of these permissions are used to collect or share personal data.

6. Data deletion

All data is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app. No backups exist on external servers.

You can delete individual habits and their history at any time directly within the app.

7. Children's privacy

Streakly is not directed at children under 13. Since no data is collected or transmitted, use of the app carries no privacy risk for minors.

8. Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. For material changes, a notice will be shown in the app.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: kontakt@streakly.app
